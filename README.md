## What is Klinko Startup Idea Validator?

Klinko Startup Idea Validator is an AI agent Skill for early-stage evaluation of a product or business concept. It examines the intended audience, the problem being solved, likely motivation, alternative behaviors, positioning assumptions, and market opportunity so founders can identify weak points and decide what evidence to gather before building further.

## What this AI agent Skill helps you do

- Clarify the audience, problem, and value proposition behind an idea
- Identify assumptions that could invalidate the concept
- Evaluate whether the proposed need is specific and meaningful
- Turn uncertainty into a practical validation plan

## Questions you can ask

- “Is this startup idea worth validating?”
- “What are the biggest assumptions behind my product concept?”
- “Who would care most about this solution and why?”
- “How should I test demand before building an MVP?”

## What you receive

| Deliverable | Decision it supports |
|---|---|
| Idea assessment | Understand the concept's current strengths and gaps |
| Audience hypothesis | Define who may care most |
| Critical assumptions | Know what could make the idea fail |
| Validation plan | Choose interviews, tests, or experiments |

## Example workflow

1. Describe the product, idea, audience, or market you want to understand.
2. Add relevant public context such as geography, category, goals, or constraints.
3. Ask a clear decision question rather than requesting generic research.
4. Review the structured answer and use follow-up questions to refine the decision.

### Example prompt

> Validate an idea for a subscription service that turns expert webinars into reusable sales and training content for small B2B companies.

### Example result structure

- A clear restatement of the idea and intended customer
- Reasons the concept may or may not create demand
- The assumptions with the highest validation risk
- Recommended evidence to collect next
